## About ROTA

ROTA is a student-led technical community at **Atatürk University** built around one principle: **the goal comes first**.

Instead of organizing people into permanent technical departments, we form dynamic teams around concrete goals — a competition, a technical problem, a product, a tool, a prototype, or another measurable outcome.

Our aim is to turn learning into practice, practice into results, and results into knowledge that can be passed on.

## How we work

> **Goal → Team → Learn & Prepare → Build or Compete → Evaluate → Share**

A team exists because there is a clear objective to pursue. Members learn what the objective requires, work together toward a tangible result, evaluate the outcome, and document what should be carried forward.

## Areas of interest

Our work may span multiple technical disciplines, including:

- Cybersecurity and CTF
- Software engineering
- Algorithms and problem solving
- Artificial intelligence, machine learning and data
- Cloud, systems and DevOps
- Embedded systems and IoT
- Open-source software and developer tools
- Hackathons and technical competitions
- Automation, platforms and applied technical projects

These are **capability areas, not permanent departments**. Teams are formed around goals.

## Engineering culture

We value **ownership, clarity, collaboration, quality, learning by doing, and continuity**.

Public repositories are used for projects and resources that are appropriate to share openly. Internal, competition-sensitive, or in-progress work may remain private until it is ready for release.
